# Reason Codes + Severity + Next Actions

## Severity scale (1–5)
- 5 = critical driver (dominant contributor to high PD or high loss)
- 4 = major driver
- 3 = moderate driver
- 2 = minor driver
- 1 = informational

## Standard reason code groups
**Credit score**
- Very low credit score (5)
- Low credit score (4)

**Affordability**
- Very high payment-to-income ratio (5)
- High payment-to-income ratio (4)

**Utilization**
- Very high revolving utilization (5)
- High revolving utilization (4)

**Delinquency**
- Multiple delinquencies last 12 months (5)
- Recent delinquency last 12 months (4)

**Credit hunger**
- Very high inquiries last 6 months (4)
- High inquiries last 6 months (3)

**Income stability**
- Unemployed (5)
- Student/Self-employed volatility (3)

**Macro stress**
- Macro under high stress (4)
- Macro stressed (3)

## Next best actions by decision
APPROVE
- approve under standard terms
- monitor early payment performance

APPROVE_REPRICE
- approve with risk-based pricing (rate increase)
- offer shorter term or smaller principal to reduce PTI

MANUAL_REVIEW
- route to manual review queue
- request documents (payslips/bank statements)
- counter-offer (reduce amount / co-applicant / guarantor)

DECLINE
- decline under policy
- offer secured alternative or seasoning period path
- provide adverse-action notice + eligibility pathway

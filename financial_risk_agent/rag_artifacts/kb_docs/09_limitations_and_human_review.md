# Limitations & Human Review Guidance

## Limitations
- Model trained on synthetic data (not production validated)
- Simplified LGD and profitability proxies
- Decision thresholds are illustrative policy settings
- Does not incorporate full bank underwriting (assets, liabilities, verified expenses)

## When human review is required
- PD in MANUAL_REVIEW band
- Any severity 5 reason code
- Conflicting signals (e.g., good score but extreme PTI)
- High macro stress environments
- Missing/uncertain documentation
